How we harmonize, and how well it works

Clean. We keep vegetation fires only (FIRMS type 0), drop low-confidence detections (MODIS confidence < 30, VIIRS “low”), and remove duplicates.

Grid. Detections are counted as fire cell-days: a 0.05° cell (~5.5 km) that burned on a given day counts once, whichever sensor saw it. This blunts the resolution gap between MODIS (1 km) and VIIRS (375 m).

Learn the conversion. On the overlap years (2012 onward) we fit, for each calendar month, how many VIIRS cell-days each MODIS cell-day is worth, separately for weak and strong (high-FRP) MODIS detections. VIIRS’ finer pixels add far more detections around small, weak fires than around big intense ones, so one flat ratio would overstate big-wildfire years. A simple monthly ratio is kept as a baseline for comparison.

Convert. MODIS-only years (2001–2011) are converted with that monthly fit, so the whole record reads in VIIRS-equivalent units. Converted months carry a 10–90% uncertainty band in the calendar tooltips.
